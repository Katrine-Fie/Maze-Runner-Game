Zombie Labyrinth

Use the arrow keys to move. Space to drop bombs, C to use chainsaw. Reach the exit, dodge zombies, and grab tools!

How to Play

  • Arrow keys to move your hero.
  • Spacebar to drop a bomb.
  • Green zombies: regular enemies.
  • Black zombies: worth extra points.
  • Running shoes make you faster for a short time.
  • Chainsaw: collect 🪚 to add to inventory. Press C to activate—10s invincibility, destroy zombies for double points!
